## Mode

Operate. A couple opens the page to add, check or share their dates; the task outranks expression.

## Surface

Single page app shell: header with names and the "Day N" counter, the next date as a large ticket, the list/month views, and the composer card (one date or milestones). Export, share, backup and import live in the footer tools.

## Committed direction

Warm keepsake: claret velvet lining as the page ground, paper tickets with ink-stamp dates, Young Serif display, Figtree body, Barlow Condensed stamp numerals. See DESIGN.md for tokens and named rules.

## Keep

- The material restraint the finish review called out: flat by default, shadows only on card, month sheet, ticket and toast.
- Themed browser surfaces (native date/time/select inputs styled to the paper ground).
- Category identity always as icon + label, never colour alone.

## Watch

- Detector contrast findings measured against white are false positives; the real ground is the claret lining.
- Month grid keyboard model (arrows, Home/End, Page Up/Down) must survive any layout change.
